Cookie policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website. They help the site function and give us reporting information.

How we use cookies

  • Essential cookies — core website functionality, secure areas, and page navigation.
  • Performance and analytics cookies — understand how visitors interact with the site so we can improve load times and user experience.
  • Functional cookies — remember your preferences for future visits.

Third-party cookies

Where we embed external media or services — social media feeds, video embeds, or event registration widgets — those third parties may set their own cookies, over which we have no direct control.

Managing your preferences

You can control or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect the performance of parts of this website.
