Environmental, Social, and Governance

Built into how we operate.

We don't just talk about ESG — we practise it. Environmental, Social, and Governance principles are built into how Juzly operates: how we source suppliers, how we structure accountability across our hubs, how we compensate our team, and how we measure our own impact. No badges without the numbers to back them.

Living framework — updated as practices evolve
Governance

Accountability by structure, not slogan.

STRUCTURE

Dual-Hub Accountability

Strategic governance and client accountability sit with our Australia HQ, structurally separated from technical delivery in Sri Lanka — so engagement ownership and execution are never the same desk. Clear chains of responsibility, not ambiguous handoffs.

GOVERNANCE

Audit Trails & Role-Based Access

Every financial transaction, procurement decision, and HR action is logged, traceable, and subject to approval workflows. Audit-ready from day one — not after the auditor arrives.

Social

People and communities, treated as stakeholders.

WORKFORCE

Fair Compensation & Growth

Top-tier compensation practices across both Australia and Sri Lanka operations. We don't arbitrage talent — we invest in it. Professional development, clear progression paths, and retention over churn. Tracked and reported through Odoo HR modules where clients want visibility.

COMMUNITY

Local Impact

We hire where we operate, source locally where possible, and direct resources toward digital literacy and community development through the Sheriff Foundation — our dedicated CSR arm.

PROCUREMENT

Supplier ESG Criteria

Our suppliers and trading partners are sourced and evaluated against ESG criteria — labour standards, ethical practices, and environmental responsibility. ESG due diligence is part of how we choose who we work with, not a post-hoc checklist.

Note on CSR vs. ESG: The Sheriff Foundation is our community-focused CSR initiative — it lives on the Impact page. ESG is the operational framework that governs how we run the business itself. Related, but not the same thing.

Environmental

Measured reduction, not marketing claims.

We don't publish "carbon-neutral" or "zero-waste" badges until we have the measurement and audit trail to back them. What we are doing now — and what we help clients implement in Odoo:

MEASUREMENT

Environmental Tracking

We track energy use, waste output, and resource efficiency across our operations. One system of record for operations and ESG — no disconnected sustainability tools.

OPERATIONS

Remote-First, Paperless Delivery

Our dual-hub model is built around remote collaboration and digitised workflows from the start. Paper-based approvals are eliminated, logistics are digitised, and unnecessary travel is reduced — governance without the footprint.

Where we are, honestly: we're early in our environmental measurement journey. We'll publish specifics as they're substantiated — not before. If you're evaluating us on environmental criteria, we'll give you the real numbers we have today and a timeline for the rest.

Reporting

What we track, what we share.

We maintain an internal ESG framework and report against it regularly — whether that's for CSRD, SASB, or emerging local regulations. ESG reporting is built into our operations, not a manual exercise every quarter. Data pipelines, dashboards, and audit trails are in place so disclosures are a byproduct of how we already work.

ESG Data Architecture

From operational inputs to audit-ready outputs — one continuous pipeline.

For procurement teams and auditors: if your vendor evaluation or audit process requires ESG disclosure, we're happy to walk you through our framework directly — and show you exactly how we configure Odoo to generate the evidence you need. We'd rather have a conversation about what we can substantiate today than hand over a document that overstates where we are.
