Odoo Integrated Enterprise Solutions

SCM

Odoo Supply Chain Solutions

Odoo Supply Chain Solutions provides a fully integrated, modular suite designed to streamline end-to-end operations from product conceptualisation to final delivery. By centralising core functions into a single system, it eliminates operational silos, ensures real-time stock visibility, and enhances procurement accuracy across the business lifecycle.

CRM

Odoo CRM

Replace the shared spreadsheet and the "who is following up with who" Slack thread with one pipeline your whole sales team actually trusts. Configured around how you sell, not a generic funnel template.

WEBSITE

Odoo Website

Corporate and product sites built on the same platform as your backend, so a form submission does not sit unread in an inbox. It lands directly in CRM as a tracked lead.

ECOMMERCE

Odoo eCommerce

An online sale updates stock and accounting in the same moment it happens. No manual reconciliation between your storefront and your warehouse.

POS

Odoo Point of Sale

One stock count across every location, not three. POS, inventory, and CRM sync live, built for retail and F&B businesses running more than one till.

RENTAL

Odoo Rental

Booking, availability, and asset-tracking that stops double-bookings before they cost you a customer. A specialist module most generalist ERP consultants rarely touch.

ESG

Odoo ESG

Track energy use, waste output, and carbon estimates inside the same system that runs your daily operations. On the ground, that means automatic ESG reporting instead of manual spreadsheet reconciliation, so your team acts on trends rather than compiling them. At a strategic level, leadership gets audit-ready disclosures, faster regulatory responses, and a single source of truth when investors or boards ask for evidence of progress.

PRODUCTIVITY

Odoo Productivity Apps

Discuss, Approvals, and Knowledge, implemented so an approval is not five email replies deep and a decision is not buried in someone's inbox. One connected workspace, not a patchwork of disconnected tools.

Why modular

Lean Enterprise Architecture

The core idea behind how we build.

Traditional enterprise systems price and provision for scale you may not need yet. Modular architecture lets you deploy only the operational core you need now, with a clear expansion path as complexity grows. It is the difference between buying a system sized for the business you are, versus the one you might become.

FactorLegacy ERPModular Odoo
Upfront costHigh capital outlayPay for modules you use
Deployment time6 to 18 months4 to 10 weeks
FlexibilityRigid, custom-code heavyConfigure, do not code
IntegrationThird-party connectorsNative app ecosystem
Scaling pathRe-platforming riskAdd modules as you grow
